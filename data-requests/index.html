Requesting the data

The Tenant Commons map shows who owns, manages and licenses rental buildings in Vancouver, and which landlord networks they belong to. You can look up any building on the map. The data behind it isn't available to download yet, but we'd like to hear from you.

Why there's no download yet

The data is still being curated and cleaned, and we haven't published our methodology. A download would present the data as finished, and it isn't. We intend to open it up once it is documented and maintained.

Until then, we share the data through conversations. That lets us explain what is solid and what isn't, and learn how it is being used.

Who we share with

  • Tenants and organizers Yes

    This project exists to close the information gap between landlords and tenants. If you are organizing your building, or against a landlord, get in touch.

  • Non-profits and academics Let's talk

    We are glad to hear about your work and to find a way to collaborate.

  • For-profit organizations No

How to ask

Write to us at info@tenantcommons.ca and tell us:

  1. Who you are: yourself and the group or organization you're with.
  2. Why you need the data: the question or problem you're working on.
  3. What it's for: what you plan to make or do with it.
  4. How it will be used: who will see the results, and where.

We'll take it from there. How the data can be used, and how it should be credited, is something we work out together in that conversation.
