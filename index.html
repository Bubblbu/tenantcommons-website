    About

    Tenant Commons is building shared, lasting infrastructure for tenant organizing. It combines public information, like City of Vancouver open data, rental licences and records released through freedom of information requests, into one place, and adds what tenants and organizers learn about who is really behind a building. It starts with who owns and manages Vancouver's rental buildings.
